About Fill the Gap

We exist to identify, understand, and bridge the practical divides in our community support infrastructure.

Our Mission & Origins

Across Newfoundland and Labrador, countless organizations and dedicated professionals do extraordinary work every day. Yet individuals and families frequently find themselves falling between the cracks of rigid criteria, lengthy waitlists, or simple navigational barriers.

Fill the Gap started with a straightforward premise: listen directly to the people experiencing the system and the frontline workers navigating it, identify exactly where the gaps occur, and build collaborative pathways to close them.

Our Long-term Commitment

Starting in St. John's allows us to listen deeply, map exact local support pathways, identify blind spots, and expand across Newfoundland and Labrador as our capacity grows.

Our Approach & Framework

WHAT WE DO

WE'RE STARTING BY LISTENING

Fill the Gap is just getting started.

Right now, our focus isn't on trying to solve every problem in our community.

It's on understanding them.

We want to hear from people who have experienced gaps in getting the help or support they needed.

  • What happened?
  • Where did you get stuck?
  • What did you need?
  • What made it difficult to get?

And most importantly:

What do you wish had been available?

WE'RE LEARNING

There are already organizations, programs, and services doing important work in our community.

Before we try to create something new, we want to understand what's already available.

We want to learn what works, what doesn't, and where people are still falling through the cracks.

WE'RE IDENTIFYING THE GAPS

As we listen and learn, we'll start to see where the biggest gaps may be.

Information Gaps

Things that can be solved simply by helping people find clear information.

Collaborative Gaps

Areas requiring direct partnership with existing organizations.

Systemic Gaps

Larger challenges that take time, resources, and deliberate planning.

We don't want to promise solutions before we understand the problem.

THEN WE'LL DECIDE WHAT WE CAN DO

This is where Fill the Gap will grow.

The core question we ask:

Is there something we can realistically do?

  • If there is, we'll work toward a practical solution.
  • If someone else is already better equipped to help, we'll point people toward them.
  • And if a gap is too large for us right now, that doesn't mean we'll ignore it. It means we'll learn from it and consider what might be possible in the future.

WHAT WE HELP WITH

Fill the Gap exists to help fill the gaps that can leave low-income people without a clear path forward.

Sometimes someone is doing everything they can to move forward, but one missing piece is standing in the way.

It may be a practical barrier during a major transition, a need that isn't covered by an existing program, or a situation where available supports don't quite meet the person's circumstances.

That's where Fill the Gap wants to help.

Our Main Focus: People in Transition

One of our primary areas of focus is supporting low-income people through significant transitions.

This may include people who are:

  • Leaving treatment
  • Transitioning out of homelessness
  • Moving from one support system to another
  • Rebuilding their lives after a major setback

Even when programs and services are available, there can still be gaps that make moving forward difficult.

Fill the Gap looks at the individual situation and asks:

“What's missing?”

When we have the ability and resources to help fill that gap, we will do our best to help.

What Might a Gap Look Like?

Every situation is different, but a gap might look like:

  • Someone leaving treatment who encounters a practical barrier that makes continuing their recovery more difficult.
  • Someone transitioning out of homelessness who has a specific need that isn't being addressed by the services available to them.
  • Someone who has been offered a job but cannot afford something essential they need to accept or start the job.
  • Someone moving from one support system to another who encounters an unmet practical need.
  • Someone trying to move forward after a major setback who has one specific barrier preventing them from taking the next step.

These are examples, not a complete list.

Treatment & Recovery Access

We want to help people facing practical barriers to accessing treatment and recovery supports.

We are not a treatment or recovery program.

Our role is to look at the barriers surrounding the person and determine what we may be able to do.

This could include helping someone understand what resources are available, offering to make a phone call, helping them take the first step toward accessing a service, or addressing a practical gap when we have the ability to do so.

Connecting People With Existing Resources

Fill the Gap does not want to duplicate services that already exist in our community.

When an existing organization or program may be able to help, we will do our best to connect people with that resource.

Depending on the situation, this may mean:

  • Helping someone identify a resource
  • Helping them understand their options
  • Offering to make a phone call
  • Helping them figure out what step to take next
  • Sharing information and resources we have available

We can't guarantee that another organization will be able to help, but we can do our best to help people find their way to the right place.

When There Is Still a Gap

Sometimes there isn't an existing service that addresses the particular need.

Sometimes a person has access to a program but is missing something practical that prevents them from moving forward.

Those are the gaps Fill the Gap is most interested in addressing.

We will look at each situation individually and determine whether there is something we can realistically and responsibly do.

More Than What's Listed Here

The situations above are examples of where Fill the Gap may be able to help. They are not a complete list.

Every person's circumstances are different, and not every gap will fit neatly into a category.

If you're facing a situation and aren't sure whether Fill the Gap can help, we encourage you to reach out.

We'll listen, look at the situation, and do our best to determine whether we can help, connect you with an existing resource, or point you toward another appropriate option.

Why We Started Fill the Gap

A reflection on lived experience, community, and having someone in your corner.

The Story Behind Fill the Gap

Fill the Gap was founded from a deeply personal experience that revealed a much broader need.

As a sister supporting her brother, our founder experienced firsthand the challenges of navigating services, finding the right resources, and understanding where to turn for support. What began as an effort to help her brother ultimately revealed a larger gap—one that many individuals and families face when trying to access the support they need.

That experience became the driving force behind Fill the Gap.

Below, our founder shares the personal story that started it all.

My brother has mental and physical challenges that mean he needs help with many aspects of his life.

We've never minded being there for him. Being family, helping him is just something we've always done.

Sometimes that means bringing him where he needs to go. Sometimes it means helping him problem-solve when something isn't working. Sometimes it means figuring out what needs to be done and finding the right person, service, or place to help get it done.

We've learned that sometimes the hardest part isn't wanting help.

It's figuring out how to get it.

There was one time when my brother really needed help with something important. We started trying to find the right place for him to get that help.

It took five phone calls before we were finally given the information we needed.

We kept going because we knew he needed it.

But it made us stop and think: What if we weren't there?

What if there was nobody to make those calls?

Nobody to bring him where he needed to go?

Nobody to help him figure out what to do next?

Nobody to help him find the service that could get the important things done?

What if he had to figure all of that out on his own?

And then we started thinking about all the other people who may not have someone in their corner.

  • People who are already struggling and don't know where to start.
  • People who don't know what services exist.
  • People who get sent from one place to another.
  • People who don't understand how to navigate the system.
  • People who are waiting for help.
  • People who may know they need something but don't know how to get from needing it to actually getting it.

We're not saying we can fix all of those problems. We can't. And Fill the Gap isn't starting with all the answers.

People shouldn't have to figure everything out alone.

What We Want Fill the Gap to Become

We want Fill the Gap to be a place that looks at those moments where people get stuck.

  • Sometimes the answer may be helping someone find an existing resource.
  • Sometimes it may be helping them understand where to start.
  • Sometimes it may be a practical barrier that needs to be addressed.
  • And sometimes we may discover a gap where something new is needed.

We don't know yet what every answer will look like. And we don't want to pretend that we do.

We want to listen to the people who are experiencing these problems. We want to learn what's already available. We want to understand what's missing. And then, as Fill the Gap grows, we want to do what we realistically can to help.

My brother had someone in his corner. He had us.

That experience made us wonder about the people who don't have that. The people who need someone to help them figure out the next step. The people who just need someone to listen and help them find a way forward.

That's where Fill the Gap begins.

We may not have all the answers today. But we're willing to listen, learn, and keep looking for them.

Because everyone deserves to be heard. Everyone deserves to be treated with dignity. And everyone deserves a chance to get the help they need.

That's why we started Fill the Gap.
