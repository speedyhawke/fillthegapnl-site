"Everyone deserves someone in their corner."

Origin

Why I Started

Fill the Gap started with something very personal to me.

My brother has had many struggles over the years, both mentally and physically. He has a really hard time trying to navigate everything on his own, so I have often been the person in his corner.

I'm the one who makes the phone calls, searches for information, figures out where he needs to go, and tries to get him the help or resources he needs.

Sometimes, it isn't as simple as making one phone call.

There have been times when I've had to call five different places just to finally reach the person who could actually help.

I've never minded doing that for my brother. He's my brother, and I want to be there for him.

But it made me start thinking about something:

What about the people who don't have someone in their corner?

What happens when someone is already overwhelmed, struggling, scared, or unsure where to turn — and they don't have a person who can make the calls, search for the information, ask the questions, and keep trying when the first door doesn't lead anywhere?

Sometimes people aren't looking for someone to fix everything.

Sometimes they just need someone to help them figure out where to start.

That's where the idea for Fill the Gap came from.

Values

What I Believe

I believe that sometimes a little compassion, a little information, and a little assistance can make something that feels impossible feel a little more manageable.

I want people to feel heard.

I want people to know that struggling to navigate a complicated system doesn't mean they've failed.

And I want to find out what we can do when there is something missing between a person who needs help and the help that already exists.

I don't have all the answers. But I'm willing to ask the questions.

And that's where Fill the Gap begins.
